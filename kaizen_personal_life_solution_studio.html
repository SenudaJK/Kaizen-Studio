Week 09 Lecture Translation

How Industrial Quality Tools Solve Personal Habits

1. Basic Seven Tools (Analytical Approach — After Numerical Data)

Used to count occurrences, visualize distributions, and eliminate variation once you start tracking:

  • Check Sheets: Structured daily habit tables & |||| tally counters for habit blockers.
  • Flow Charts: Standardizing your Morning & Evening sequences to reduce decision fatigue.
  • Pareto Diagram (80/20): Ranking friction tallies so you solve the 20% of triggers causing 80% of missed habits.
  • Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagram: Mapping root causes across Environment, Energy, Routine, and Mindset instead of blaming "willpower".
  • SPC Control Charts: Plotting your natural baseline (UCL/LCL) vs. your target Specification Limits.
  • Scatter Diagram: Proving correlations between upstream habits (e.g., Sleep vs. Focus).
2. New Seven Tools (Proactive Approach — Verbal Data Before Execution)

Used to organize ideas, predict future obstacles, and plan complex goals before failure happens:

  • PDPC (Process Decision Program Chart): A pre-mortem tree that anticipates "What could go wrong?" (e.g., rainy morning, late dinner) and locks in backup SOPs in advance.
  • Arrow Diagram: Critical path sequencing for multi-week life goals.
